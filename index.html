Introduction

Mb Alegria ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Ma, Ano Ulam? mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

When you use the App, we may collect the following information:

  • Meal Plans: Dishes you plan for your weekly meals
  • Pantry Data: Ingredients you track in your pantry, including quantities and expiration dates
  • Saved Dishes: Dishes you save as favorites
  • Grocery Lists: Shopping lists you create from meal plans
  • App Preferences: Your settings and preferences within the App

Automatically Collected Information

We may automatically collect certain information when you use the App:

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: Features you use, time spent in the App, interactions with content
  • Crash Reports: Technical information about app crashes or errors

Payment Information

When you purchase a Premium subscription:

  • Payment processing is handled by Google Play and RevenueCat
  • We do not store your credit card or payment information
  • We receive confirmation of your subscription status from RevenueCat
  • RevenueCat may collect transaction data as described in their Privacy Policy

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Provide App Functionality: Store and sync your meal plans, pantry data, and grocery lists
  • Personalization: Provide dish suggestions based on your pantry ingredients
  • Premium Features: Manage your subscription status and provide Premium features
  • App Improvement: Analyze usage patterns to improve features and user experience
  • Technical Support: Diagnose and fix technical issues
  • Communication: Send important updates about the App or your subscription

Data Storage and Security

Local Storage

Most of your data (meal plans, pantry items, grocery lists) is stored locally on your device using SQLite database. This data remains on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

Cloud Storage

Currently, the App does not sync data to cloud servers. All data is stored locally on your device.

Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information:

  • Data is stored in encrypted format on your device
  • We use secure connections (HTTPS) for any network communications
  • Payment processing is handled by trusted third-party services (Google Play, RevenueCat)

However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services that may collect information:

RevenueCat

We use RevenueCat to manage in-app purchases and subscriptions. RevenueCat may collect:

  • Purchase transaction data
  • Subscription status
  • Device identifiers

RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Google Play Services

The App uses Google Play Services for app distribution and in-app purchases. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data Retention

  • Meal Plans, Pantry Data, Grocery Lists: Stored on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App
  • Subscription Data: Retained by RevenueCat according to their data retention policy
  • Usage Data: Retained for up to 90 days for analytics purposes

Your Rights

Access and Control

  • View Your Data: All your data is accessible within the App
  • Edit Your Data: You can modify meal plans, pantry items, and grocery lists at any time
  • Delete Your Data: You can delete individual items or clear all data within the App

Data Deletion

To completely delete all your data:

  1. Open the App
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Select "Clear All Data"
  4. Uninstall the App to remove all local data

Subscription Management

To manage or cancel your Premium subscription:

  1. Open Google Play Store
  2. Go to Subscriptions
  3. Select Ma, Ano Ulam?
  4. Manage or cancel your subscription

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.
